About the Shoe

50 Years of Heritage.
Still the One.

01

Premium Suede Upper

Soft yet structured, the suede upper moulds to your foot over time. Originally designed for the court — now one of the most recognisable silhouettes in streetwear history.

02

T-Toe Construction

The signature T-shaped toe overlay isn't just aesthetic — it was built to reinforce the shoe under the pressure of handball play. Decades later, it remains the detail that defines the Spezial.

03

Flat Gum Rubber Sole

Flexible, grippy, and ageless. The gum sole gives you enough contact with the ground to feel every surface — from parquet floors to polished pavement. Breaks in beautifully after the first few wears.
